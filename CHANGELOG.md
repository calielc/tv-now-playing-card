# Changelog

From 1.1.0 on, every merge into `main` makes a release, and its notes are on the
[releases page](https://github.com/calielc/tv-now-playing-card/releases).
The versions before that are listed below. The format follows
[Keep a Changelog](https://keepachangelog.com/en/1.1.0/) and the project uses
[Semantic Versioning](https://semver.org/).

## [1.0.1] - 2026-11-27

### Changed

- Without a cover, the card is compact and uses the app's brand colour as the background.
- Without a media title, the app name is shown once, as the title.

## [1.0.0] - 2026-11-27

### Added

- Card with the cover as background, app icon and name, state badge, title, series or artist, and a progress bar that moves every second.
